Why CarePath

Built to close the gap between visits

CarePath helps reduce communication barriers between doctors and patients. Critical information stays organized and accessible, tailored to each hospital, so care teams can make quicker, more accurate decisions.

Sign in as a patient, healthcare provider, or hospital admin to access your dashboard.

What's inside

One platform, two views

Patients and care teams see the same record, shaped around what each of them needs to do next.

🧑‍⚕️

Built for patients

  • Upcoming appointments front and centre, with symptom and severity tracking
  • Personal info card — blood type, age, sex, and health card number
  • Draggable, zoomable map to find and select your main care facility
  • One-tap directions and a searchable list of nearby facilities
  • Book appointments with date, time, reason, and severity level
🏥

Built for care teams

  • Comprehensive, filterable patient list by hospital
  • Autocomplete for patient search, diagnoses, and medications
  • Text-to-speech for patient summaries and clinical notes
  • User-friendly dropdowns built for fast, accurate charting
  • Light / dark mode and accessibility tools on every page
Security & compliance

Your data, handled carefully

CarePath is designed around the same expectations Canadian patients already have for their health information.

🔒

Encryption

Passwords are hashed, and sensitive fields are protected in transit and at rest.

🍁

PIPEDA-aligned

Built with Canadian privacy principles in mind — collect only what's needed, and tell people why.

🛡️

Access control

Role-based views mean patients, providers, and admins only ever see what's relevant to them.
